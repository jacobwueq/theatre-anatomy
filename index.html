    Walk at eye height以視線高度步行

    Walk starts where you are looking. WASD to walk, drag to look, Shift to run, Esc to stop. You climb stairs and steps but walls stop you.步行由你正在看的位置開始。WASD 行走，拖曳環顧，Shift 跑，Esc 停止。可以上落樓梯和台階，但會被牆擋住。

      Controls操作方法

      Left-click to select, double-click to operate. Right-drag to orbit, middle-drag to pan, scroll to zoom towards the cursor, double-middle-click to re-centre. WASD moves, Q/E down/up, Shift faster. H is the hand tool (drag to pan); O switches between orbiting and turning in place. G runs the next cue, P pauses the machinery. Esc leaves any mode.左鍵選擇，雙擊操作。右鍵拖曳轉視角，中鍵拖曳平移，滾輪向游標縮放，雙按中鍵設為中心。WASD 移動，Q/E 升降，Shift 加速。H 是抓手工具（拖曳平移）；O 切換環繞與原地轉視角。G 執行下一個提示，P 暫停機械。Esc 離開任何模式。
